# Four-perspective review

These are structured self-review perspectives performed during development, not independent expert endorsements, judicial opinions, or external peer review.

| Perspective requested | Assessment | Evidence / unresolved issue |
|---|---|---|
| Student / researcher | The package can be studied and rerun without paid APIs. | CLI, readable modules, explicit sources, three-seed outputs; owner should reproduce and explain every pipeline before presenting it. |
| Professor / supervisor | Suitable baseline engineering, insufficient for a novel-paper acceptance claim. | Tests protect key leakage routes; external baselines, stronger datasets, uncertainty analysis, and novelty review remain. |
| Industrialist / operator | Offline batch tools work for the documented schemas. Production adoption is premature. | Input checks and review flags exist; no monitoring, live data connectors, calibration guarantee, operational trial, or service-level measurement. |
| Judge / critical reviewer | Claims must be bounded by the observed evidence. | Real executions and hashes are recorded. Simulated astronomy is labelled, phishing is not called multimodal, citation support is not called truth, and self-review is not called external review. |

## Decisions

1. Keep all measured failures. Do not retune on exposed test outputs and represent that as clean held-out performance.
2. Do not claim three completed UTAS research programmes, three accepted papers, novelty, industrial deployment, or plagiarism certification.
3. Preserve dataset credits and source attribution. Raw data remain separately downloaded.
4. Hold the research-publication gate until contemporary baselines, independent validation, and a demonstrable contribution are available.
5. Hold the production gate until realistic prevalence, operating costs, calibration, and prospective monitoring have been evaluated.

## Known failure cases

- Exact vector grouping cannot identify different feature vectors from the same phishing campaign.
- Median filling can overstate robustness if missingness is informative or correlated differently in deployment.
- Lexical citation overlap can miss a negated claim or a wrong entity with nearly identical wording.
- ALCE examples from 200 questions cannot establish performance across all academic disciplines.
- PLAsTiCC simulation assumptions and rare-event prevalence differ from real alert streams.
- A fixed top-5% candidate count is not a telescope scheduling policy.
- Repeated split results share data; they are not independent replications.
