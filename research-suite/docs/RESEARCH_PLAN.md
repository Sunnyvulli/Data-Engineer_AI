# From runnable baselines to defensible research

## Delivered milestone

Three independently implemented CPU pipelines, explicit fetch commands, data-hash manifests, data-split safeguards, reproducible CLI evaluation, offline scoring, tests, and measured reports. This milestone is complete when the recorded commands and checks pass. It is a baseline engineering release, not the completion of the advertised university research programmes.

## Publication gates

1. Conduct a current systematic related-work search, record inclusion criteria and publication dates, and articulate a specific unaddressed question. Do not infer novelty from a project advertisement.
2. Register a fixed hypothesis, primary metric, comparison models, data splits, exclusion policy, and compute budget before further evaluation. Baseline results already viewed here cannot be reused as an untouched final test.
3. Acquire a stronger, permissioned evaluation corpus. Phishing needs raw modalities plus provenance; citation triage needs out-of-domain and contradiction tests; astronomy needs independent cadences and real observations.
4. Reproduce specialised baseline systems within their licenses. Compare fairly under identical input availability and compute constraints.
5. Quantify uncertainty with appropriate paired or cluster-aware resampling; report all seeds, ablations, failures, and null results. Separate hyperparameter development from final evaluation.
6. Write the paper only around actual measured contributions. State simulation, sample size, dataset age, and deployment limitations. Follow the venue disclosure rules. Confirm all human authors' contributions and approval.

No external emails, supervisor enquiries, paper submissions, or authorship invitations were sent as part of this release.
